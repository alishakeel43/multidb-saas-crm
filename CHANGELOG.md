Adds `updated_at` indexes on the inbox conversation and message tables.
- Delete a whole WhatsApp, Messenger, or Instagram chat from the Unified Inbox with a "Delete chat" button in the chat header. Every message's content and locally stored attachments are removed and the chat leaves the list, tab counts, and folders; message rows stay as tombstones so webhooks and Messenger/Instagram history sync never re-import them. If the customer writes again, the chat reappears with only the new messages. Linked CRM records and notes are kept, and the customer's copy on Meta is unchanged. Requires the new `cleared_at` migration.
- Delete WhatsApp, Messenger, and Instagram messages from the Unified Inbox via a hover action on each bubble.
